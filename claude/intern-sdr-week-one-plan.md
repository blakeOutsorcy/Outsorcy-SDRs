## Context

  - Interns are in Prishtina, Kosovo; Blake (MT) and Travis (US) are online from 16:00 Prishtina.
  - Interns finished sales and AI training, go live Monday Oct 5 on calls, email and LinkedIn.
  - A mock call platform exists for practice.
  - Stack: Zoho CRM (telephony, cadences), HeyReach (LinkedIn), Clay lists, Slack.

## Goal

Five days of live outbound per intern, everything logged in Zoho same day, graded mock baseline by Friday. Meetings are a bonus in week one; KPIs are reps and quality.

## Daily rhythm (Prishtina time; ET is minus 6, MT is minus 8 through Oct 24)

  - 10:00 to 10:30 start of day, read feedback, post plan in Slack
  - 10:30 to 12:00 mock call block (8 to 10 calls, self-scored, flag best and worst)
  - 12:00 to 13:00 research top 15 accounts
  - 13:00 to 13:45 lunch
  - 13:45 to 15:00 LinkedIn and email
  - 15:00 to 16:00 live call block 1 (Eastern)
  - 16:00 to 16:20 standup with Blake and Travis (10:00 ET, 8:00 MT)
  - 16:20 to 18:30 live call block 2 (Eastern and Central), live coaching
  - 18:30 to 19:00 wrap, log Zoho, post scorecard

Mountain and Pacific prospects out of scope in week one.

## Day by day

  - Mon: setup and first dials. Tool check, mock opener scenario, kickoff at 15:00, managers demo 5 live calls, interns dial 20 with listen-in.
  - Tue: full rhythm. Mock gatekeeper and voicemail, 25 LinkedIn requests, enroll 50 in cadence, first recorded call review.
  - Wed: objections. Mock built from top 2 real objections; mid-week data check, swap list or number if connect rate under 3%.
  - Thu: volume. Mock closing a hesitant prospect, LinkedIn follow-ups, longest call block, peer recording review.
  - Fri: certify and reset. Certification mock graded by Blake or Travis, Zoho cleanup, 30 min retro, week two targets.

## Dial ramp per intern

Mon 20, Tue 40, Wed 50, Thu 60, Fri 50 (220 for the week). Week two floor 60 a day, moving toward 60 to 100.

## Daily scorecard (target by Thursday / benchmark / red flag)

  - Connects: 4 to 5 / 5 to 8% of dials / under 3% two days running
  - Conversations (2+ min): 2 to 3 / about half of connects / zero over two days
  - Meetings: bonus / 10 to 15% of conversations, 1 to 2 per intern for the week
  - Voicemails: 15 to 20
  - Cadence enrollments: 25 a day after Tuesday's 50 / bounce over 5% is a flag
  - Email replies: track / 2 to 5%
  - LinkedIn requests: 25 a day; accept rate 25 to 35%, under 15% is a flag
  - Mock calls: 8 to 10 a day, average 3.5 or higher, under 3.0 by Wed is a flag
  - Zoho logged same day: 100%

## Mock call program

Rubric (1 to 5 each): opener, permission, relevance hook, problem question, value statement (quality vs offshore, half-cost only vs domestic hires), objection handling, close, tone and pace. Gate: 3.5 average on Monday's opener scenario before first live dial. Scenarios: Mon cold open and permission; Tue gatekeeper and voicemail; Wed top 2 real objections; Thu closing a hesitant prospect; Fri certification with random persona.

## Async accountability

Three Slack posts per intern per day: 10:00 plan, 15:00 solo block done (mock scores, recording links, LinkedIn sent), 19:00 scorecard. Blake and Travis review the two flagged recordings and the Zoho dashboard before standup and leave one line of feedback per intern. Dedicated channel (e.g. #intern-sdr). Consider a Prishtina teammate (Fatjon or Shpat) as on-site contact for tool blockers.

## Meeting definition and handoff

Counts when on a calendar with accepted invite, US company hiring or planning to hire for a role Outsorcy places, decision maker or direct influencer. Handoff note on the Zoho lead same day: company/contact/title/LinkedIn, role and headcount, trigger, how they staff today, budget signal, objections, channel that booked it. Intern sends confirmation, day-of reminder, owns reschedules.
