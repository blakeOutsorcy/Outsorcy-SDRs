## How to use it

  - Interns: your assigned accounts are the rows where Owner is your name, already copied into your accounts.md. Research them, log every touch in Zoho, keep your prospect notes in interns/<name>/prospect-notes.md.
  - Managers: to reassign, change the Owner cell here and move the row between the two interns' accounts.md files. To assign more from Tier B or Round 3, add rows here with an Owner and add them to that intern's accounts.md. Keep the suppression list below current.
  - Claude: when an intern asks "what are my accounts," read their accounts.md. Never suggest an account that is on the suppression list, owned by someone else here, or in another intern's accounts.md.

## Adding your own accounts

  - Say "Add [company] to my list" in your chat. Claude checks, in order: suppression list, Zoho, the Owner column here, every intern's accounts.md. First claim wins.
  - If it clears, it goes under Self-added in your accounts.md with the angle it fits and a one-line reason. If it fits neither angle, Claude adds it with "angle: manager review" and you raise it at the 16:00 standup.
  - Unassigned Tier B rows (in the workbook) and Round 3 seeds below can be claimed the same way.
  - Managers can remove a self-added row or move the company to suppression. Everyone sees every list, so if you want a company, claim it before someone else does.
