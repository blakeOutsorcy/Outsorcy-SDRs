# Claims ledger

Who owns which company. One row per company. This is the only ownership list: if a company is in an intern's section, it is theirs. Zoho holds the people, the cadence state and the call history; this file holds the claim.

Rules
  - First claim wins. A company listed here belongs to that intern until a manager moves it.
  - Interns edit only their own section. Managers edit anything.
  - Rows are added by the split-list and enroll skills, never typed by hand from memory.
  - A parked company comes back to the same intern when it shows up again in a daily job-post list. It does not go back into a cadence before that.

Status values
  - claimed: on the intern's plate from a daily list, Leads not yet enrolled
  - active: Leads enrolled in a cadence
  - parked: out of cadence, waiting for a fresh job post
  - meeting: meeting booked, handed to Blake or Travis
  - closed: not qualified, not interested, or do not call. Do not re-enroll.

Backfilled Oct 29, 2026 from Zoho Lead ownership. Leads = number of intern-owned Leads at the company. Older research for Diar's accounts lives in interns/diar/prospect-notes.md and archive/diar-accounts-oct-2026.md.
